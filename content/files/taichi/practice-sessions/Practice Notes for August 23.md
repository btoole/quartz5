Practice Notes for August 23
Great job training in this heat.
I’m always hoping to find simple, immediately applicable techniques for unbalancing an opponent, but they are hard to come by. Ultimately, relaxing and standing with correct posture is likely the most direct path.

・Cock the wrist (15 degrees)
・Use of the palm (bend fingers back and return them)
・Make a fist, cock the wrist, and move using the point of contact
・Focus on the fingertips (index and middle fingers)
・Tuck the chin in and support the back of the head to align the neck correctly
Was there anything else?
