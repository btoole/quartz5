One can imagine large swaths of art history being populated by art that was largely concerned with the good, with the beautiful only part of a convenient delivery vehicle. To the extent that it ever existed it has now been largely forgotten. Didactic, sentimental, easily explained - all qualities that for art to have currency today are still quite valuable - changing from being vehicles for religious dogma to being financial instruments in and of themselves.
