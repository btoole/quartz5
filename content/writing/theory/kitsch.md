Imagine a line bounded at the far left end by sensation and at the far right end by sentiment. Kitsch lives entirely on the right side. This zone  includes all varieties of sentiment no matter how mediated by any variety of tropes \[ irony for example ]. Most art lives here - from Jean-Léon Gérôme and Lawrence Alma-Tadema in the 19th century to figures like Pierre Bonnard in the early 20th century and Jeff Koons and Frank Stella in the late 20th century.
